
Key Events in India (January – October 2026)


Major National & Governance Milestones

• Republic Day & Chief Guests: India celebrated its 77th Republic Day on January 26, 2026, with European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen and European Council President António Costa as chief guests.
• Independence Day Theme: The 80th Independence Day on August 15, 2026, was observed under the theme "150 Years of Vande Mataram" and "Yuva Shakti for Viksit Bharat@2047".
• Aadhaar Mascot: The Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) launched the official Aadhaar mascot 'Udai' on January 8, 2026.
• Paperless Judiciary: Kerala inaugurated India's first fully paperless district judiciary in Kalpetta.

Economy, Industry & Infrastructure

• Economic Growth: India's real GDP growth for 2026 is projected around 6.9% by major analysts like Goldman Sachs.
• Wholesale Inflation: India's wholesale inflation reached an 49-month high of 11.39% by mid-October 2026.
• Trade & Manufacturing: India's exports to China experienced a 40% growth with the trade deficit crossing $100 billion. India also emerged as the first country to commercially produce bio-bitumen for sustainable road construction.
• Energy & Tech: India Energy Week 2026 took place in Goa (January 27–30), and the AI Impact Summit India 2026 was held in February 2026 alongside regional AI conferences in Rajasthan.

Environment & Ecology

• Wetlands & Biodiversity: Two new wetlands were officially designated as Ramsar sites in October 2026. A new reed snake species (Calamaria mizoramensis) was also discovered in Mizoram.

Awards & Culture

• Jnanpith Award: Vairamuthu was selected for the 60th Jnanpith Award, following Vinod Kumar Shukla who received the 59th Jnanpith Award.
• International Achievements: India secured four gold medals at the International Chemistry Olympiad 2026
