
Major Events of 2013 at a Glance

• Natural Disasters:
	• Severe flash floods and landslides struck Uttarakhand (June 15–17, 2013), causing catastrophic loss of life around Kedarnath and the Mandakini river basin.
	• Cyclone Phailin hit the coasts of Odisha and Andhra Pradesh in October, prompting a massive evacuation of over 1.3 million people.
• Accidents & Tragedies:
	• An illegal seven-story building collapsed in Shil Phata, Thane (April 2013), killing 74 people.
	• Fatal stampedes occurred at the Allahabad railway station (February) and near the Ratangarh Mata Temple in Datia, Madhya Pradesh (October).
	• Serial blasts targeted a BJP rally in Patna addressed by Narendra Modi in October.
• Space & Science:
	• The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) successfully launched the Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan) on November 5, 2013.
• Legislation & Politics:
	• Parliament passed major reform legislation, including the National Food Security Act and the Land Acquisition Act.
	• The Union Cabinet cleared the creation of Telangana as India's 29th state in October 2013
