• Wrestlers' Protest: Prominent Indian wrestlers (including Vinesh Phogat, Sakshi Malik, and Bajrang Punia) protested at Jantar Mantar against Wrestling Federation of India (WFI) chief Brij Bhushan Sharan Singh over sexual harassment allegations.

February

• Delhi Excise Policy: Deputy Chief Minister Manish Sisodia was arrested by the CBI regarding irregularities in the scrapped Delhi excise policy.
• Weather Extremes: Central India recorded a 99% rainfall deficit (the worst in 122 years).

March

• Rahul Gandhi Disqualification: Rahul Gandhi was disqualified from the Lok Sabha following a criminal defamation conviction.
• Assembly Elections: The BJP won the Tripura Assembly elections and returned to power in Meghalaya and Nagaland as a coalition partner.

April

• Population Milestone: India officially surpassed China to become the world’s most populous country, estimated at 142.86 crore.
• Atiq Ahmed Killed: Gangster-turned-politician Atiq Ahmed and his brother Ashraf were shot dead live on television by assailants posing as journalists in Prayagraj.
• Lithium Reserves: Large reserves of lithium were discovered in Jammu and Kashmir.

May

• Manipur Clashes: Ethnic and violent clashes broke out in Manipur following a Tribal Solidarity March, resulting in widespread displacement and casualties.
• New Parliament Building: Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated the new Parliament building in New Delhi.

June & July

• Odisha Train Collision: A tragic three-train crash occurred in Balasore, Odisha, causing massive loss of life and injuries.
• France-India Ties: PM Narendra Modi attended Bastille Day celebrations in France as the Guest of Honour.

August

• Chandrayaan-3: ISRO successfully landed the Chandrayaan-3 Vikram lander on the South Pole of the Moon, making India the first nation to do so.
• Haryana Violence: Communal violence flared up in Nuh district of Haryana during a VHP-led procession.

September

• G20 Summit: India successfully hosted the G20 Leaders' Summit in New Delhi under the theme "Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam" ("One Earth, One Family, One Future").
• Women’s Reservation Bill: Parliament passed the Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam (Women's Reservation Bill), reserving 33% of seats for women in the Lok Sabha and state legislative assemblies.

October

• Aditya-L1: ISRO launched Aditya-L1, India's first space-based solar observatory mission to study the sun.
• Article 370: The Supreme Court of India delivered a landmark verdict unanimously upholding the abrogation of Article 370 in Jammu and Kashmir.

November

• Silkyara Tunnel Rescue: All 41 trapped construction workers were safely rescued after 17 days following a tunnel collapse in Uttarkashi, Uttarakhand.
• Cricket World Cup: India reached the final of the ICC Men's Cricket World Cup 2023, finishing as runners-up after losing to Australia
