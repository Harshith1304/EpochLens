
Year in Review: Major Events in India (2018)


⚖️ Legal & Judicial Landmarks

• Section 377 Decriminalized: In a historic verdict on September 6, the Supreme Court of India unanimously struck down the colonial-era law, decriminalizing consensual gay sex.
• Aadhaar Verdict: The Supreme Court upheld the constitutional validity of Aadhaar but significantly limited its scope, declaring it unnecessary for bank accounts, school admissions, and mobile connections.
• Sabarimala Verdict: The apex court ruled that women of all ages must be allowed entry into the Sabarimala temple in Kerala, stating that selective exclusion violates fundamental rights.
• Judiciary Crisis: Four senior judges of the Supreme Court held an unprecedented press conference to air public grievances regarding the administration and allocation of cases by the Chief Justice.

🏛️ Politics & Governance

• Statue of Unity Inauguration: Prime Minister Narendra Modi unveiled the world's tallest statue (182 meters)—a monument dedicated to India’s first Home Minister, Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, in Gujarat.
• Farmers' Marches: Tens of thousands of farmers participated in massive protest marches across Mumbai and New Delhi, demanding farm loan waivers and fair crop pricing.
• Renaming Drive: The Uttar Pradesh government executed a major renaming initiative, officially changing Allahabad to Prayagraj and Faizabad to Ayodhya.

🚀 Science & Technology

• ISRO Milestone: The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) successfully launched its 100th satellite using the PSLV-C40 carrier vehicle.
• Aviation History: Flying Officer Avani Chaturvedi became the first Indian woman to fly a fighter jet solo, piloting a MiG-21 Bison.

📈 Business & Economy

• Walmart-Flipkart Acquisition: U.S. retail giant Walmart acquired a 77% controlling stake in Indian e-commerce pioneer Flipkart for $16 billion, marking the world's largest e-commerce acquisition at the time.
• $100 Billion Milestones: Both Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) and Reliance Industries (RIL) crossed the elite $100 billion market capitalization threshold.
• PNB Scam Uncovered: A massive multi-crore financial fraud involving celebrity jeweler Nirav Modi was unearthed at the Punjab National Bank.

🌪️ Natural Disasters

• Kerala Floods: During August, Kerala suffered its worst monsoon flooding in nearly a century, resulting in over 400 casualties and displacing hundreds of thousands of citizens.
• Cyclone Gaja: A severe cyclonic storm hit the coastal regions of Tamil Nadu in November, causing heavy infrastructure damage and agricultural loss.

🏆 Sports Achievements

• Under-19 Cricket World Cup: The Indian cricket team, coached by Rahul Dravid, clinical won the ICC U-19 World Cup in New Zealand.
• Blind Cricket World Cup: India retained their title by defeating Pakistan in a thrilling final held in Sharjah.
• Hima Das Sprint: Hima Das became the first Indian track athlete to win a gold medal at a global event during the World U20 Championships.

🕯️ Major Demises

Atal Bihari Vajpayee: The former Prime Minister of India and co-founder of the BJP passed away on August 16.
M. Karunanidhi: The veteran DMK patriarch and former Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu passed away on August 7.
Sridevi: The legendary Indian cinema superstar passed away unexpectedly in Dubai on February 24.
