• 18th Lok Sabha Elections: Held between April and June, registering a record-breaking 642 million voters (including 312 million women). Prime Minister Narendra Modi took the oath of office for a historic third consecutive term, leading a coalition government under the NDA banner.
• Jammu & Kashmir Assembly Elections: Conducted for the first time in a decade following the abrogation of Article 370, resulting in the formation of a new alliance government.
• Electoral Bonds Verdict: In February, a unanimous landmark judgment by a constitutional bench of the Supreme Court of India struck down the Electoral Bond Scheme as unconstitutional and arbitrary.
• Uniform Civil Code (UCC): Uttarakhand became the first state in independent India to pass a Uniform Civil Code bill.
• Judicial Overhaul: The three new criminal justice laws—Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam—officially replaced the colonial-era IPC, CrPC, and Indian Evidence Act.

🚀 Science & Aerospace

• XPoSat & Aditya-L1: ISRO commenced the year by launching the X-ray Polarimeter Satellite (XPoSat) to study black holes, followed quickly by successfully inserting the Aditya-L1 spacecraft into Lagrange Point 1 to observe the solar corona.
• GaganYaan Milestone: ISRO officially announced and introduced the four chosen astronaut-designates for India’s premier human spaceflight mission.
• Meteorological Advancements: The launch of the INSAT-3DS satellite in February substantially enhanced the country's weather forecasting, storm warning, and climate monitoring capabilities.

📈 Economy, Infrastructure & Trade

• Atal Setu Inauguration: India's longest sea bridge, the Mumbai Trans Harbour Link (Atal Setu), spanning 21.8 km, was officially opened to public transit.
• Macroeconomic Resilience: India maintained its status as the world’s fastest-growing major economy, with GDP growth hovering near 6.5–7% alongside stable, low core inflation.
• Digital Infrastructure Boost: Unified Payments Interface (UPI) volumes breached record-setting peaks, exceeding 16.58 billion transactions in a single month.
• Green Energy Expansion: Construction escalated on the Khavda Renewable Energy Park in Gujarat, cementing its trajectory to become the world’s largest hybrid solar-wind installation.

🏆 Sports & Culture

• T20 World Cup Triumph: The Indian Men's Cricket Team won the ICC T20 World Cup in June, remaining completely undefeated throughout the tournament and breaking an 11-year ICC trophy drought.
• Youngest World Chess Champion: 18-year-old Grandmaster D. Gukesh won the FIDE Candidates Tournament and subsequently clinched the World Chess Championship title.
• Paris 2024 Olympics: India concluded its campaign with 6 medals, highlighted by shooter Manu Bhaker becoming the first Indian athlete since Independence to win two medals at a single Olympic edition.
• Ram Mandir Pran Pratishtha: The formal consecration ceremony of the Ram Mandir in Ayodhya took place in January, marking a major cultural shift and driving massive tourism infrastructure growth in Uttar Pradesh.
