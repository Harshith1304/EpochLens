
Key Major Events in India (2016)

• Demonetization: On November 8, 2016, Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced the demonetization of ₹500 and ₹1,000 banknotes to curb black money.
• Uri Attack & Surgical Strikes: In September 2016, militants attacked an army camp in Uri, Jammu and Kashmir, killing 19 soldiers; India responded with surgical strikes across the Line of Control.
• Pathankot Attack: Terrorists attacked the Indian Air Force base in Pathankot in January 2016.
• Rio Olympics: PV Sindhu won a silver medal in badminton for India.
• ISRO Launch: ISRO successfully launched multiple satellites using a single rocket.
• Telecom Expansion: Reliance Jio launched its 4G telecom network with free/cheap promotional plans.
• Passing of J. Jayalalithaa: Former Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu, J. Jayalalithaa, passed away in December 2016
