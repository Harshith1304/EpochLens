
Major Events in India (2020 Timeline)

The year 2020 was a historic and tumultuous year for India, heavily defined by the COVID-19 pandemic, significant geopolitical standoffs, major policy shifts, and key judicial conclusions.

1. The COVID-19 Pandemic & Lockdowns

• First Case: On January 30, 2020, India recorded its first official COVID-19 case in Thrissur, Kerala, involving a student returning from Wuhan, China.
• Janata Curfew: On March 22, 2020, India observed a voluntary 14-hour nationwide lockdown called by Prime Minister Narendra Modi to prepare the public for social distancing.
• Nationwide Lockdown: On March 24, 2020, the Government of India ordered a strict 21-day nationwide lockdown for its 1.3 billion citizens. This was subsequently extended in multiple phases through May.
• Migrant Exodus: The abrupt halt of transport and economic activity triggered a humanitarian crisis, prompting 40 million migrant workers to travel thousands of kilometers back to their hometowns on foot or via special trains.
• Economic Relief: The government launched the Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan (Self-Reliant India Movement) in May to inject economic stimulus and structural reforms into a heavily impacted market.

2. Geopolitical & Defense Developments

• Galwan Valley Clash: Tensions peaked along the Line of Actual Control (LAC) in eastern Ladakh. During the night of June 15–16, 2020, a violent hand-to-hand combat in the Galwan Valley resulted in the deaths of 20 Indian Army personnel.
• Chinese App Ban: In response to border escalations and security concerns, the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology banned 59 popular Chinese mobile applications, including TikTok, WeChat, and CamScanner.
• UN Security Council: On June 17, 2020, India was elected as a non-permanent member of the United Nations Security Council (UNSC) for a two-year term starting in January 2021.

3. Political & Legislative Milestones

• Bodo Peace Accord: On January 27, 2020, the Government of India signed a historic tripartite agreement with the Government of Assam and Bodo representatives, successfully ending a 50-year-old extremist crisis.
• Delhi Riots: In late February 2020, severe communal violence erupted in Northeast Delhi amid tensions surrounding the Citizenship Amendment Act (CAA), resulting in over 40 casualties.
• Agricultural Farm Laws: In September 2020, Parliament passed three contentious agricultural reform bills. This sparked widespread, long-term protests and blockades by farmer unions at the borders of New Delhi.
• Ram Mandir Foundation: On August 5, 2020, Prime Minister Narendra Modi laid the foundation stone for the construction of the Ram Janmabhoomi temple in Ayodhya, following the 2019 Supreme Court verdict.

4. Disasters and Transportation Events

• Cyclone Amphan: In May 2020, an extremely severe cyclonic storm hit West Bengal and parts of Odisha, causing massive structural damage and claiming dozens of lives.
• Vizag Gas Leak: A hazardous styrene gas leak occurred at the LG Polymers chemical plant near Visakhapatnam on May 7, 2020, resulting in over 10 fatalities and thousands falling ill.
• Air India Express Flight 1344: On August 7, 2020, a repatriation flight operating under the Vande Bharat Mission overshot the tabletop runway at Calicut International Airport in Kerala, breaking apart and killing 21 people.

5. Major Legal Judgments & Closures

• Nirbhaya Case Verdict: After years of legal delays, the four convicts of the 2012 Delhi gang-rape and murder case were executed by hanging at Tihar Jail on March 20, 2020.
• Babri Masjid Demolition Case: On September 30, 2020, a special CBI court acquitted all 32 accused individuals—including veteran political figures like L.K. Advani and Murli Manohar Joshi—citing a lack of conclusive evidence regarding a pre-planned conspiracy.
