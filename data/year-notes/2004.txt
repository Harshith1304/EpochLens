
. Political Upheaval & The General Election

• April–May 2004: India held its 14th Lok Sabha general elections in four phases, using electronic voting machines fully nationwide for the first time.
• The "India Shining" Upset: The ruling Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP)-led National Democratic Alliance (NDA) called early elections, confident in their ["India Shining"](1.1.7, 1.3.8) economic campaign. However, the alliance faced a shock defeat due to rising concerns over job scarcity and rural economic distress.
• Sonia Gandhi’s Renunciation: In May, despite leading the Congress-led United Progressive Alliance (UPA) to victory, Sonia Gandhi stunned the nation by declining the Prime Ministership.
• Dr. Manmohan Singh Sworn In: On May 22, 2004, renowned economist Dr. Manmohan Singh was sworn in as India's 13th Prime Minister, heading the new UPA coalition government.
• State Assemblies: Regional shifts took place as the Congress party won major state elections, notably forming a new government under Y.S. Rajasekhara Reddy in Andhra Pradesh.

🌊 2. The Indian Ocean Tsunami Disaster

• December 26, 2004: A massive 9.1 magnitude earthquake off the coast of Sumatra, Indonesia, triggered a catastrophic tsunami across the Indian Ocean.
• Devastation: The tsunami struck India’s southeastern coast and islands with wave heights reaching up to 12 meters. Over 10,000 people were killed, and thousands more went missing or were displaced.
• Impacted Regions: The Union Territory of the Andaman and Nicobar Islands, alongside mainland coastal areas in Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and Pondicherry, suffered the worst brunt of the disaster.

🚀 3. Science, Technology & Space

• September 2004: The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) successfully launched GSAT-3, popularly known as EDUSAT. It was the first Indian satellite built exclusively to service the educational sector, bringing interactive distance classroom environments to remote and rural areas.

🕊️ 4. Foreign Policy & National Security

• Indo-Pak Peace Process: In June, after bilateral talks on nuclear confidence-building measures, India and Pakistan agreed to establish a dedicated hotline to avoid accidental nuclear risks and maintain a moratorium on nuclear testing.
• Internal Security: Government operations led to a notable drop in militancy incidents in Jammu & Kashmir compared to the prior year. Meanwhile, domestic security focus shifted toward containing Naxalite operations across central and eastern states.

⚖️ 5. High-Profile Crimes & Legal Actions

• The End of Veerappan: On October 18, 2004, the notorious forest bandit and sandalwood smuggler, Koose Munisamy Veerappan, was shot dead by the Tamil Nadu Special Task Force in Operation Cocoon, ending a decades-long multi-state manhunt.
• Kanchi Shankaracharya Arrest: In November, Jayendra Saraswathi, the prominent seer of the Kanchi Kamakoti Peetham, was arrested in connection with a high-profile murder case, sparking massive national media coverage and religious debate.

🏏 6. Sports Milestones

• Historic Tour of Pakistan: In March and April, the Indian cricket team led by Sourav Ganguly visited Pakistan for a historic full-fledged cricket series. India won both the One Day International (ODI) series and clinched their first-ever Test series victory on Pakistani soil.
• Olympic Silver: At the 2004 Athens Olympics, Major Rajyavardhan Singh Rathore won a Silver Medal in the men's double trap shooting event, securing India’s first-ever individual Olympic silver medal.
