
 Legislative & Policy Milestones

• Right to Information (RTI) Act: Enacted in October 2005, giving ordinary citizens the legal power to request information from public authorities, radically boosting public transparency.
• MGNREGA Enactment: The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act was passed, legally guaranteeing 100 days of wage employment per year to rural households.
• Value Added Tax (VAT): Introduced on April 1, 2005, across most Indian states to streamline domestic trade taxation.

🤝 Diplomacy & International Relations

• Kashmir Bus Service: On April 7, the first bus service in 60 years commenced between Srinagar and Muzaffarabad across the Line of Control.
• India–US Nuclear Pact: Initial frameworks and diplomatic dialogues were established, laying the foundation for the historic civil nuclear cooperation agreement.

🌪️ Natural Disasters & Public Tragedies

• The Great Mumbai Floods: In July 2005, unprecedented monsoon rains dumped 94 cm of precipitation within 24 hours, bringing the financial capital to a standstill and claiming hundreds of lives.
• Mandher Devi Temple Stampede: A horrific stampede occurred in January in Maharashtra, leading to over 200 fatalities.
• Kashmir Earthquake: On October 8, a catastrophic 7.6 magnitude earthquake hit the region, necessitating major cross-border disaster relief operations.

🚂 Transportation & Infrastructure

• Valigonda Train Derailment: A flash flood washed away a bridge in Andhra Pradesh in October, causing a derailment that resulted in 77 fatalities.
• Vadodara Train Crash: A severe collision between a passenger train and a goods train occurred in Gujarat.

🎬 National Culture & Cinema

• Dadasaheb Phalke Award: Renowned director Adoor Gopalakrishnan was honored with India's highest cinema award.
• IFFI Goa: The 36th International Film Festival of India was permanently institutionalized in Goa with veteran actor Dev Anand opening the event.
