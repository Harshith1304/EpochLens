
Year in Review: 2021 Major Events in India


🗓️ January

• January 1: India officially began its two-year tenure as a non-permanent member of the United Nations Security Council (UNSC).
• January 2: The Drug Controller General of India approved emergency use authorization for two COVID-19 vaccines: Covishield (Oxford/AstraZeneca produced by Serum Institute) and Covaxin (Bharat Biotech).
• January 16: India launched the world’s largest COVID-19 vaccination drive, initially targeting healthcare and frontline workers.
• January 26: On Republic Day, the year-long farmers' protests escalated as a tractor rally breached the Red Fort in New Delhi, leading to widespread clashes.

🗓️ February

• February 7: A catastrophic glacier burst in the Chamoli district of Uttarakhand triggered severe flash floods down the Rishiganga and Dhauliganga rivers, leaving over 200 people dead or missing.
• February 24: The Motera Stadium in Ahmedabad was renamed the Narendra Modi Stadium, becoming the largest cricket stadium in the world.

🗓️ April – May

• April – May: India was struck by a devastating second wave of COVID-19 driven by the Delta variant. The surge overwhelmed the healthcare system, leading to critical, country-wide shortages of medical oxygen and hospital beds.
• May 1: Phase 3 of the vaccination drive opened up eligibility to all citizens above the age of 18.
• May 17: Cyclone Tauktae, an extremely severe cyclonic storm, struck the western coast of India, causing heavy casualties and damage in Gujarat, Maharashtra, and Goa.
• May 23: Days after Tauktae, Cyclone Yaas hit the eastern coast, devastating coastal areas of Odisha and West Bengal.

🗓️ June – July

• June 18: Legendary Indian sprinter Milkha Singh (famously known as the "Flying Sikh") passed away at age 91 due to COVID-19 complications.
• July 7: Dilip Kumar, one of the greatest icons of Hindi cinema, passed away at the age of 98.
• July 7: Prime Minister Narendra Modi executed a massive Cabinet overhaul, introducing 36 new faces and seeing high-profile exits, including Health Minister Harsh Vardhan and IT Minister Ravi Shankar Prasad.

🗓️ August

• August 7: Neeraj Chopra won a historic Gold Medal in Men's Javelin Throw at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics, securing India’s first-ever Olympic gold medal in track and field.
• August 6: The Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna Award, India's highest sporting honor, was officially renamed the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award.

🗓️ October

• October 8: Tata Sons won the competitive bidding process to acquire national carrier Air India for ₹18,000 crore, bringing the airline back to its original founders after 68 years.
• October 21: India crossed a monumental public health milestone by administering 1 billion (100 crore) COVID-19 vaccine doses.

🗓️ November

• November 19: In a major political reversal on Guru Nanak Jayanti, Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced the repeal of the three controversial farm laws, leading to the official end of the year-long farmers' blockade around Delhi.
• November 29: Parag Agrawal, an IIT Bombay alumnus, was appointed as the new Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Twitter.

🗓️ December

• December 8: India's first Chief of Defence Staff (CDS), General Bipin Rawat, his wife, and 11 other military personnel tragically lost their lives when their Mi-17V5 helicopter crashed in Coonoor, Tamil Nadu.
• December 13: India’s Harnaaz Sandhu was crowned Miss Universe 2021 in Eilat, Israel, bringing the title back to India after 21 years.
