
 Politics & Governance

• New Leadership (July-August): Ram Nath Kovind was elected as the 14th President of India, succeeding Pranab Mukherjee. Shortly after, M. Venkaiah Naidu was sworn in as the 13th Vice-President.
• State Elections (March-December): The BJP expanded its political footprint significantly, securing a landslide victory in Uttar Pradesh (where Yogi Adityanath became Chief Minister), alongside wins in Uttarakhand, Goa, Manipur, Himachal Pradesh, and Gujarat. The Congress party won Punjab.
• Cabinet Reshuffle (September): Nirmala Sitharaman was appointed as the Defence Minister, becoming only the second woman to hold the post after Indira Gandhi.

💰 Economy & Structural Reforms

• The GST Rollout (July 1): India launched the Goods and Services Tax (GST), executing the country's most significant indirect tax reform since independence by replacing a complex web of central and state taxes with a unified system.
• Ease of Doing Business (October): Driven by structural changes, India jumped an unprecedented 30 spots to break into the top 100 in the World Bank’s Ease of Doing Business index.
• Demonetization Aftermath: The economy experienced a brief deceleration in growth (dipping to a multi-year low of 5.7% in the mid-quarter) as small businesses and the automotive sector adjusted to the cash crunch from the late-2016 banknote ban.

⚖️ Landmark Legal Judgments

• Right to Privacy (August): A 9-judge bench of the Supreme Court unanimously declared the Right to Privacy as a Fundamental Right under Article 21 of the Constitution.
• Triple Talaq Banned (August): The Supreme Court ruled the practice of instant triple talaq (Talaq-e-Biddat) unconstitutional and void.
• Electoral Banning (January): The apex court prohibited the use of religion, race, caste, community, or language to seek votes during elections.

🚀 Science, Technology & Infrastructure

• ISRO's World Record (February 15): The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) made history by successfully launching 104 satellites in a single mission using the PSLV-C37 rocket.
• Inauguration of Mega Projects: Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated the Chenani-Nashri Tunnel in Jammu & Kashmir (India's longest highway tunnel at the time) and Phase 1 of the high-tech Hyderabad Metro.

🌍 Defence & Geopolitics

• Doklam Standoff (June–August): A tense, 73-day military face-off occurred between Indian and Chinese armed forces over Chinese road construction in the Doklam plateau near the Bhutan border. It was successfully resolved via diplomatic withdrawal.
• ICJ Victory (November): India scored a massive diplomatic win when judge Dalveer Bhandari was re-elected to the International Court of Justice after the UK withdrew its candidate.

🏆 Culture, Society & Sports

• Miss World 2017 (November): Manushi Chhillar won the Miss World pageant, bringing the blue crown back to India after a 17-year hiatus.
• FIFA U-17 World Cup (October): India hosted its first-ever FIFA tournament, the FIFA U-17 World Cup, which catalyzed massive soccer infrastructure updates across major cities.
• Entertainment & Weddings (December): The Indian film industry saw historical box-office records shattered by Baahubali 2: The Conclusion. In December, cricketer Virat Kohli and actor Anushka Sharma wed in Tuscany, Italy
