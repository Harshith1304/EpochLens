
2015 Chronology: Major Events in India


January – February: Governance & Elections

• January 1: The Government of India formally established the NITI Aayog (National Institution for Transforming India), completely replacing the 65-year-old Planning Commission.
• January 22: Prime Minister Narendra Modi launched the flagship "Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao" (Save the Daughter, Educate the Daughter) and Sukanya Samriddhi Account welfare schemes.
• January 26: US President Barack Obama attended India's Republic Day parade as the Chief Guest, marking the first time a US president filled this role.
• January 31: India successfully test-fired its first canister-launched version of the Agni-V Intercontinental Ballistic Missile (ICBM) from Wheeler Island.
• February 10: The Aam Aadmi Party (AAP), led by Arvind Kejriwal, secured a historic landslide victory in the Delhi Legislative Assembly elections, winning 67 out of 70 seats.

March – April: Legal Milestones & Emergencies

• March 24: The Supreme Court of India struck down Section 66A of the Information Technology Act, declaring it unconstitutional and protecting freedom of speech online.
• March 27: Former Prime Minister Atal Bihari Vajpayee was awarded the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honor.
• March 28: Saina Nehwal became the first Indian woman shuttler to attain the World No. 1 rank in badminton.
• April 1–11: The Indian Armed Forces executed Operation Raahat, successfully evacuating over 4,640 Indian nationals and 960 foreign citizens from war-torn Yemen.
• April 25: A massive 7.9-magnitude earthquake devastated Nepal, causing severe tremors across North and East India and resulting in cross-border casualties.

May – June: Regional Shifts & Diplomacy

• May 11: The Karnataka High Court acquitted Tamil Nadu Chief Minister J. Jayalalithaa in her long-running disproportionate assets case, paving the way for her political return.
• June 6: India and Bangladesh signed a historic Land Boundary Agreement, ending a decades-old border dispute by exchanging 162 enclaves.
• June 21: The First International Yoga Day was celebrated globally; PM Modi led a massive gathering of over 35,000 people at Rajpath in New Delhi.

July – September: Digital Push & Space Breakthroughs

• July 1: Prime Minister Narendra Modi officially inaugurated the Digital India campaign to enhance online infrastructure and digital literacy.
• July 27: Terrorists attacked a police station in Gurdaspur, Punjab, killing seven people before the three attackers were neutralized.
• July 27: Former President and esteemed scientist Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam passed away at the age of 83 after collapsing during a lecture in Shillong.
• September 28: ISRO successfully launched Astrosat, India's first dedicated multi-wavelength space observatory, using the PSLV-XL rocket.

October – December: Climatic & Political Climax

• October: Tensions escalated across the nation following the tragic Dadri lynching incident over rumors of beef possession, triggering widespread social debates.
• November 8: The Mahagathbandhan (Grand Alliance) of JD(U) and RJD defeated the BJP-led alliance in the Bihar Legislative Assembly elections, returning Nitish Kumar as Chief Minister.
• November – December: Unprecedented heavy monsoon rains triggered the catastrophic 2015 Chennai Floods, which claimed over 400 lives and caused billions in infrastructure damage.
• December 25: In an impromptu diplomatic move, PM Modi made a surprise stopover in Lahore to meet Pakistani Prime Minister Nawaz Sharif.

Summary of Key Economic & Policy Indicators (2015)


Category	Core Development / Milestone
National Economy	The Union Budget abolished the wealth tax and increased service tax to 14%. GDP growth hit 7.3%.
Banking & Finance	Mudra Bank was launched to fund small businesses, and commercial licenses were granted to Bandhan Bank and IDFC.
Defense Reform	The government formally approved the implementation of the long-awaited One Rank, One Pension (OROP) scheme.
