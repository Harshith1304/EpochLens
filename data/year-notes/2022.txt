
Summary of Major Events in India (2022)


🏛️ Governance, Politics & Identity

• First Tribal Woman President: Droupadi Murmu made history by assuming office as the 15th President of India.
• State Legislative Elections: The BJP won key assembly elections across 6 out of 7 states (including Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Goa, and Manipur), while the Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) registered a landslide victory in Punjab.
• Azadi Ka Amrit Mahotsav: The country marked 75 years of Indian independence with mass public campaigns like Har Ghar Tiranga.
• The Agnipath Scheme: A new short-term military recruitment system was launched, triggering widespread student and youth protests across several states.

⚖️ Legal & Judiciary Milestones

• Abortion Law Liberalization: In a landmark verdict, the Supreme Court ruled that all women—regardless of marital status—are legally entitled to safe abortions up to 24 weeks.
• 50th Chief Justice: Justice D.Y. Chandrachud took oath as the Chief Justice of India.
• Hijab Ban Upheld: The Karnataka High Court upheld restrictions on religious attire in schools, ruling that wearing the hijab is not an essential religious practice under Islam.

🚀 Defense, Science & Technology

• Indigenous Military Capabilities: India commissioned its first home-built aircraft carrier, INS Vikrant, significantly expanding its naval sovereignty.
• Private Space Boom: Skyroot Aerospace launched Vikram-S, marking India's first successful privately developed rocket mission into space.
• The 5G Rollout: The government successfully executed a massive 5G spectrum auction grossing over ₹1.5 lakh crore, paving the way for the high-speed telecom revolution across major cities.

🌦️ Climate & Tragedies

• Morbi Bridge Collapse: A British-era suspension bridge in Gujarat tragically collapsed over the Machchhu River, claiming 135 lives.
• Extreme Weather Waves: Northern parts of the country experienced breaking heatwaves that affected staple crop yields, while record-breaking monsoon cloudbursts caused devastating floods in Assam and Uttarakhand.

🏆 Sports Highlights

• Historic Chess Olympiad: For the very first time, India hosted the 44th FIDE Chess Olympiad in Chennai.
• Pay Parity in Cricket: The BCCI announced an equal-pay policy, offering identical match fees to both men and women international cricket players.
