• Delhi Assembly Elections (February): The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) won a decisive majority in the Delhi Legislative Assembly, securing 48 out of 70 seats.
• Manipur Political Shift (February): Following persistent ethnic tensions, Chief Minister N. Biren Singh resigned, leading to the temporary imposition of President's Rule.
• Waqf (Amendment) Act, 2025: Parliament passed controversial legislative amendments to the Waqf Act, prompting nationwide public debate and localized protests, notably in West Bengal.
• Judicial Transitions (November): Justice Surya Kant was sworn in as the 52nd Chief Justice of India (CJI), succeeding Justice B.R. Gavai.
• Bihar Assembly Elections (November): The BJP-led National Democratic Alliance (NDA) secured a landslide victory, winning 202 out of 243 seats.

💰 Economy & Corporate

• Union Budget 2025–26: The government announced a major structural update, offering a complete income tax rebate for earnings up to ₹12 Lakh per annum under the new tax regime.
• Semiconductor Breakthrough (September): India presented its first functional batch of "Made in India" semiconductor microchips to Prime Minister Modi, completing a rapid 3.5-year cycle from policy approval to physical fabrication.
• GST Rate Overhaul (September): The GST Council compressed tax slabs into a simplified system (primarily 5%, 18%, and a 40% sin tax), which pushed collections to record highs.
• Monetary Policy Easing: The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) cut the repo rate by a cumulative 125 basis points over four consecutive cycles, lowering it to 5.25%.

🛡️ Geopolitics & Security

• Operation Sindoor (May): Following a tragic terror strike in Pahalgam that claimed 26 lives, the Indian Armed Forces executed cross-border counter-terrorism strikes against camp infrastructures. A temporary bilateral military de-escalation ceasefire followed later in the month.
• Left-Wing Extremism Drawdown: The Ministry of Home Affairs reported a historic reduction in the operational footprint of Left-Wing Extremism (LWE), with active high-violence zones falling to just 11 districts nationwide.
• US Tariff Fractures (August): The newly formed US administration imposed blanket 25% tariffs on Indian goods, escalating later to secondary penalties aimed at capping Indian imports of Russian crude.

🚀 Science & Infrastructure

• Space Docking Success (January): ISRO successfully executed the SPADEEX mission, docking two individual satellites in orbit—a milestone enabling autonomous space power transfers and laying the groundwork for the future Bhartiya Antariksh Station.
• Anji Khad & Chenab Rail Bridges (April): India's first cable-stayed railway bridge (Anji Khad) and the world's highest railway bridge (Chenab) were fully integrated into the Udhampur–Srinagar–Baramulla Rail Link (USBRL) network.
• Aviation Expansion: Phase 1 operations officially commenced at both the Noida International Airport (Jewar) and the Navi Mumbai International Airport, dramatically reducing historical hub congestion.

🏏 Sports & Culture

• Maha Kumbh Mela (January–February): The historic Prayagraj congregation drew over 660 million devotees to the Triveni Sangam. However, the gathering faced significant crowd-control issues, including a tragic crush that claimed over 30 lives.
• ICC Men's Champions Trophy (March): The Indian Men's Cricket team lifted the Champions Trophy after defeating New Zealand in a tense final in Dubai.
• ICC Women's Cricket World Cup (November): The Indian Women's Cricket team clinched their historic, maiden ODI World Cup title.
• Cricket Retirement (May): Legendary batsman Virat Kohli officially announced his retirement from Test Cricket.

⚠️ Significant Domestic Crises & Accidents

• Air India Flight 171 Tragedy (June): An Air India flight crashed shortly after takeoff from Ahmedabad Airport, resulting in 260 fatalities including people on the ground.
• Aviation Crew Crisis (December): IndiGo Airlines was forced to cancel over 4,500 flights within a 10-day period following scheduling failures linked to strict compliance with the DGCA's updated Flight Duty Time Limitations (FDTL).
