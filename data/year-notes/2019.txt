
Year in Review: 2019 Landmark Events in India


📅 First Quarter (January – March)

• 10% EWS Reservation Approved (January 7): The Union Cabinet approved a 10% reservation quota in central government jobs and educational institutions for the Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) among the general category.
• The Pulwama Terror Attack (February 14): A suicide bomber targeted a convoy of the Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) in Pulwama, Jammu & Kashmir, resulting in the tragic loss of 40 jawans.
• The Balakot Airstrike (February 26): In a retaliatory counter-terror operation, the Indian Air Force (IAF) conducted precision airstrikes on a Jaish-e-Mohammed training camp in Balakot, Pakistan.
• Mission Shakti (March 27): India successfully conducted its first anti-satellite (A-SAT) missile test, destroying a live satellite in Low Earth Orbit (LEO) and becoming the fourth nation with this capability.

📅 Second Quarter (April – June)

• 2019 Lok Sabha Elections (April 11 – May 19): India conducted the world's largest democratic exercise across seven polling phases, recording its highest-ever historical voter turnout.
• Landslide Victory & Modi 2.0 (May 23): The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) won a decisive single-party majority with 303 seats (353 for the NDA alliance), securing a second consecutive term for Prime Minister Narendra Modi.
• Formation of Jal Shakti Ministry (May): The government consolidated its water-related administrative bodies into the single unified Ministry of Jal Shakti to streamline water management and launch the Jal Jeevan Mission.

📅 Third Quarter (July – September)

• Chandrayaan-2 Launch (July 22): The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) launched its second lunar exploration mission, featuring an orbiter, lander (Vikram), and rover (Pragyan).
• Abrogation of Article 370 (August 5): The Parliament revoked the special status granted to Jammu & Kashmir under Article 370. The state was officially reorganized into two distinct Union Territories: Jammu & Kashmir, and Ladakh.
• Banning of Triple Talaq (August 1): The Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Marriage) Act was passed, officially criminalizing the practice of instant triple talaq.
• Final Assam NRC List (August 31): The final National Register of Citizens (NRC) list for Assam was published, validating over 3 crore residents and excluding roughly 19 lakh people.
• Corporate Tax Rate Cut (September 20): In a major boost to revive economic growth, Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman slashed base corporate tax rates for domestic companies from 30% to 22%.

📅 Fourth Quarter (October – December)

• Opening of the Kartarpur Corridor (October 24): India and Pakistan signed a historic agreement to open a visa-free transit corridor, allowing Indian Sikh pilgrims access to the Gurdwara Darbar Sahib in Pakistan.
• The Ayodhya Verdict (November 9): A unanimous 5-judge bench of the Supreme Court resolved a decades-old land dispute, awarding the contested Ayodhya site to a trust for a Hindu temple and allocating an alternative 5 acres to the Sunni Waqf Board for a mosque.
• Citizenship Amendment Act passed (December 11): Parliament passed the Citizenship Amendment Act (CAA), offering an accelerated path to citizenship for persecuted religious minorities from Pakistan, Bangladesh, and Afghanistan, which sparked widespread nationwide protests.
• Appointment of Chief of Defence Staff (December 30): The government created the post of Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) to ensure seamless tri-service integration, appointing General Bipin Rawat as India's first CDS.

🏆 Notable Sports & Culture Milestones in 2019

• Badminton History: P.V. Sindhu became the first Indian ever to win gold at the BWF Badminton World Championships by defeating Nozomi Okuhara.
• Nobel Prize: Indian-born economist Abhijit Banerjee was jointly awarded the 2019 Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences for his experimental approach to alleviating global poverty.
